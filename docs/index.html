PL Forecast Bench

A benchmark of forecasts for the Polish power system: national wind and solar generation, demand and the day-ahead price, day-ahead (issued before the SDAC gate closure) and intraday (1 to 6 hours ahead), at 15-minute resolution. Every model sees only what was public at the moment of the forecast. Test year: October 2025 to September 2026.

Live track

Every month after the frozen test year, the reference models that run on a CPU (naive, PSE plan, ridge, gradient boosting) forecast the new month with the same tasks and as-of rules, refitted on the previous 12 months, and are scored here by a scheduled job. Foundation models are not part of it (they need a GPU), and the live months are not part of benchmark v1. Same task as the tabs above; MAE, skill against the reference below it.

Rules

  • Day-ahead: one forecast per delivery day D issued on D-1 at 10:00 Polish time, two hours before the SDAC gate closure (12:00), for every quarter-hour of D.
  • Intraday: a forecast at every full hour for the quarter-hours starting 1 to 6 hours later (20 intervals). No intraday price task: the day-ahead price of D is known on D-1.
  • As of the origin: actual wind, PV and demand count as public 60 minutes after their interval ends; the SDAC price and the PSE plan at the publication time PSE reports; a weather run of ECMWF IFS 7 hours after its initialisation. Weather input is an archived forecast, never a reanalysis such as ERA5.
  • Splits: train 21 Jun 2024 to 30 Jun 2025, validation Jul to Sep 2025, test Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. Models may be refitted at any origin on data public at that origin; the reference models refit quarterly.
  • PSE plan day-ahead is published on D-1 between 13:30 and 20:30, after the gate, so it has more information than the rules allow. It is the reference for skill but is not ranked. Intraday it is as of the origin and ranked.
  • Value metrics use a simplified settlement and should not be read as a statement of the current market rules, which have to be verified: imbalance cost = the forecast traded at the SDAC price, deviation settled at the single imbalance price CEN; battery = 1 MW / 2 MWh MILP schedule on the forecast price, settled at the actual price.

Submit

Forecasts as CSV or Parquet with columns track, variable, origin_utc, target_utc, point and optionally q10 to q90. Score them locally and open a pull request with the scores, a meta.json and a link to the forecast file and the code:

plfb pse && plfb nwp && plfb build && plfb verify
plfb evaluate --forecast my_test.parquet --name my-model --meta meta.json --out submissions/my-model
plfb leaderboard
